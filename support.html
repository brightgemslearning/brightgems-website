Support

We're a small team and we read every message. Most questions get a reply within two business days.

Contact us

Email hello@brightgems.app. It helps to include your device (for example, iPhone 15 or iPad), the app version, and what happened.

Subscriptions and billing

Bright Gems subscriptions are handled by Apple, so Apple manages billing, cancellations and refunds.

  • Cancel: open Settings on your iPhone or iPad, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Bright Gems. Or, in the app, open Grown-ups, tap "Change or cancel the subscription" and answer the grown-ups question.
  • Refund: request one from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Restore a purchase on a new device: sign in with the same Apple ID, tap Plans at the top of the home screen, answer the grown-ups question, then tap Restore purchases.

Common questions

My child finished their 10 free questions. What now?

On the free plan, 10 new questions unlock each day. Your child keeps every gem and level they've earned.

How do I find the parent report?

Tap Grown-ups at the top of the home screen. The Thinking Skills Check report and the weekly summary are there.

Can I delete our data?

Yes. Bright Gems keeps your child's progress on your device only. There's no account, and nothing is sent to us unless you choose to send it (for example, puzzle notes or the activity record). To delete it, open Grown-ups and tap Edit, then Delete player, or tap Start over to erase a player's progress (both ask the grown-ups question). Or simply delete the app. If you've emailed us or sent us notes or a record and want them deleted, write to hello@brightgems.app. See our privacy policy for details.

I found a mistake in a puzzle.

Thank you! Email us a screenshot and we'll fix it in the next update.
