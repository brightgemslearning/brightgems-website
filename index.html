Questions parents ask

What ages is Bright Gems for?

Kids aged 6 to 10 (Grades 1–4). Puzzles adapt to each child's level, so it grows with them.

How long should my child play each day?

About 10 minutes. It's designed as a short daily workout, not an endless game.

Which devices does it work on?

Bright Gems is coming to the Apple App Store first. Android may follow later.

Does it have ads?

No. There are no ads and no third-party trackers in the app.

What information do you keep?

Only what the app needs to work and to build your parent report. Read our privacy policy for the details.

How much does it cost?

Bright Gems is free to download, with 10 questions a day. The full plan is US$44.99 a year or US$8.99 a month, starts with a 7-day free trial, and covers up to 4 children. Prices in Canada are shown in CAD in the App Store.

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open Settings on your iPhone or iPad, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Bright Gems.
