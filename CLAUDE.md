# Bright Gems Website (brightgems.app)

## What this repo is
The public website for **Bright Gems**, a learning brand for children ages 6–10 (Grades 1–4).
It is the home for two product lines:
1. **The app**: *Bright Gems: Logic for Kids* (iPhone/iPad, App Store).
2. **Bright Gems Books**: CogAT practice workbooks sold on Amazon (KDP), with more reasoning-skills workbooks planned.

Goal: grow brightgems.app into a **premium education brand site** covering both the app and the books.

## How the site is built and hosted
- Plain **static HTML/CSS**. Keep it that way: no frameworks or build tools unless the owner approves.
- Hosted on **Netlify**. Pushing to the main branch deploys the live site.
- Domain and DNS are managed in **Cloudflare**. Do not change DNS or email settings.

## Product facts to use (keep consistent everywhere)
- Audience: parents of children ages 6–10. Parents pay, kids play.
- Markets: **United States and Canada, in English**. Copy must **not read as obviously Canadian** (the books target US families).
- App pricing: **US$44.99/year with a 7-day free trial** (main offer), or **US$8.99/month**. Covers up to **4 child profiles**.
- Free plan: free download, **10 questions a day** plus **one free assessment**.
- Key feature: an **adaptive assessment** (starts easy, gets harder) with a detailed parent report and recommendations.
- Game world: **Gem Island**: kids collect awards that unlock new places to explore.
- Contact: hello@brightgems.app (customers), admin@brightgems.app (business).

## Writing rules
- Talk about "thinking skills": logic, patterns, numbers, spatial reasoning.
- **Never** claim IQ gains, higher test scores, or that a child will get into a gifted program.
- Warm, clear, trustworthy tone for parents. No hype, no pressure tactics.
- Children must never be targeted with sales messages.

## How to work with the owner
- The owner has **no coding background**. Explain changes in plain words.
- **No live changes without approval.** Work on a branch and show what changed before anything is merged to main.
- Never add tracking scripts, ads, analytics or third-party embeds without explicit approval.
- Never post, send, publish or spend money on the owner's behalf.
