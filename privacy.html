Privacy policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

The short version

  • We collect only what the app needs to work and to build your parent report.
  • No ads, no third-party trackers, and no selling or sharing of anyone's information for marketing.
  • We never ask your child for their full name, photo, address, phone number or email.
  • You can ask us to see, correct or delete your family's information at any time.

This policy explains how Bright Gems ("we", "us") handles information in the Bright Gems: Logic for Kids app (the "app") and on this website, brightgems.app. The app is made for children ages 6 to 10 and is designed to be set up and managed by a parent or guardian.

Information we collect

Child profiles. A parent creates a profile for each child, with up to 4 children on one plan. A profile holds a nickname you choose and your child's grade, so puzzles start at the right level. Please use a nickname or first name only.

Learning activity. As your child plays, the app records their answers, progress, levels, gems and Surprise Treats, and the results of each assessment. We use this to choose the next puzzles and to create your parent report.

Parent information. If you contact us or set up parent features, we may have your email address. We use it only to support your account and answer your questions.

Purchases. Subscriptions are bought and managed through Apple. Apple handles payment, and we never see or store your card or bank details. Apple tells us whether a subscription is active so the app can unlock the full plan.

Messages to us. If you email hello@brightgems.app, we keep your message and our reply so we can help you.

What we don't collect or do

  • We don't show ads, and we don't use advertising networks or third-party tracking or analytics tools in the app.
  • We don't ask children for personal information such as their full name, photo, voice, location, address, phone number or email.
  • There is no chat, and children can't share anything publicly or contact other users.
  • We don't sell, rent or trade anyone's information, and we don't use it to build advertising profiles.

How we use information

  • To run the app, match puzzles to your child's level and save their progress.
  • To create assessment results and parent reports.
  • To check whether your subscription is active.
  • To answer support questions and fix problems, such as a mistake in a puzzle.
  • To keep the app and website secure and working.

Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers who help us run Bright Gems, and only what they need to do that job. They include Apple (app distribution, purchases and, if you allow it on your device, crash reports), Netlify (hosts this website), and our email providers (to receive and reply to your messages). We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the safety of children or other users.

Children's privacy

Bright Gems is built for Apple's Kids Category. Purchases and links that leave the app sit behind a parent check. We collect only the information described above, which is what the app needs to work. We don't require a child to give more information to keep playing.

Parents can, at any time:

  • ask to see the information we hold about their child,
  • ask us to correct it or delete it, and
  • tell us to stop collecting more of it, which may mean their child can no longer use some features.

To do any of these, email hello@brightgems.app from the address linked to your account, or include enough detail for us to find it. We may ask a question to confirm you are the parent before we act.

How long we keep it

We keep your family's information while you use the app. If you ask us to delete it, we delete it within 30 days, except anything we must keep by law. Information stored only on your device is removed when you delete the app.

Keeping it safe

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information, and we limit access to people who need it to run Bright Gems. No system is perfectly secure, so please contact us right away if you think something is wrong.

This website

brightgems.app doesn't use cookies, ads or analytics tools. Our host, Netlify, keeps standard server logs, such as IP addresses and pages requested, to deliver and protect the site.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, including the United States (for example, under the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act and state privacy laws) and Canada, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your information, and to know how it is used. We'll honor these requests for every family, wherever you live. We never sell personal information.

Information may be processed in Canada, the United States or other countries where our service providers operate.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version here and update the effective date. If a change affects how we use children's information, we'll ask for a parent's consent first where the law requires it.

Contact us

Questions or requests about privacy: hello@brightgems.app
